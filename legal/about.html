About Us

SPACED is built by Joepiter, a Netherlands-based sole trader (eenmanszaak)

We built SPACED because we believe every self-coached athlete deserves the same quality of training a paid coach would give them — without the €150–300/month price tag.

Why we started

Most serious amateur athletes — Hyrox competitors, runners, functional fitness athletes — coach themselves. They piece together workouts from YouTube, forums, and guesswork, without a clear plan that adapts to how their training is actually going.

A real coach solves that, but costs more than most people are willing or able to pay every month. SPACED exists to close that gap: an AI coach that knows your sport, your goal, your race date, and your recent sessions, and adjusts your plan the moment you need it to — not on the coach's schedule, but on yours.

What we do

SPACED generates a complete, periodized training plan from a short intake, then keeps adapting it as you log sessions and talk to your AI coach. No generic templates, no one-size-fits-all programs — the plan is built around your sport, your equipment, your limitations, and the date you're training toward.
