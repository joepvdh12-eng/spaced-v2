Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  |  Effective: July 2026

We keep this simple: you own your data, we use it only to make SPACED work for you, and we never sell it.

1. Who We Are

SPACED is operated by Joepiter, a sole trader (eenmanszaak) registered in the Netherlands with the Chamber of Commerce (KVK). You can reach us at: privacy@spaced.app

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth)
  • Profile data: your name, sport, fitness goal, training level, days per week, and race date
  • Training data: workout schedules, completion logs, effort ratings, and notes you add
  • Coach messages: the messages you exchange with the AI coach
  • Payment data: subscription status and Stripe customer ID — we never store card numbers
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used (to improve the product)

3. Why We Use Your Data (Legal Basis)

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): to deliver the service you subscribed to
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): to improve the product, prevent fraud, and keep the service secure
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): for marketing emails — you can unsubscribe at any time

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We only share data with third-party services that are necessary to operate SPACED:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — database and authentication, hosted in the EU (Ireland)
  • Anthropic (anthropic.com) — AI coach responses. Your messages are processed but not used to train their models under our API agreement
  • Stripe (stripe.com) — payment processing. Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified
  • Netlify (netlify.com) — hosting and serverless functions

We do not sell your data to anyone.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Active account: as long as your account exists
  • After account deletion: 30 days (to allow recovery), then permanently deleted
  • Payment records: 7 years (Dutch tax law requirement)

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

As an EU resident, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@spaced.app. We respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies:

  • Necessary cookies: session authentication (no consent required)
  • Analytics cookies: only with your consent

You can manage cookie preferences via the banner on our site.

8. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. We use Row Level Security in our database, meaning your data is only accessible to your account. We regularly review our security practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We'll notify active subscribers by email if we make material changes to this policy. The latest version is always available at this URL.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@spaced.app
